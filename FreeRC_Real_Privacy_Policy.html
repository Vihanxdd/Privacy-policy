Privacy Policy – FreeRC Real

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeRC Real ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the FreeRC Real mobile application.

1. Information that may be handled

Depending on the features you use and how the app is configured, information may include:

  • Account details such as your email address, username, and account or user ID.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • App activity needed to operate rewards, Star Token balances, offers, and redemption requests.
  • Messages and details you send to customer support.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide app features, maintain reward balances and redemption records, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, maintain security, and troubleshoot or improve the service.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

FreeRC Real uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, and protect advertising, subject to the applicable settings, consent choices, and Google policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Firebase and other service providers

If Firebase services are enabled in the app, information may be processed by Google Firebase to provide functions such as account authentication, database storage, app operation, or diagnostics. Only the Firebase services actually used by the published app should be described here.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Sharing of information

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the app, including advertising, hosting, database, and technical-support providers, where those providers are actually used. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others. Confirm that this description matches the app's actual practices.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve disputes or protect the service. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can contact us to ask about your information or request account deletion. Email freercsupport@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request” and include the account email or username needed to identify your account. Do not send your password. We may need to verify that the request is yours.

Before publishing, make sure the app and its public listing provide the account-deletion process required for the services and app-store rules that apply to FreeRC Real. Explain any data that must be retained and why.
